  
\# Request for Proposal (RFP) Structure: IT Consulting

\#\# 1\. Project Overview & Background  
\*   \*\*Executive Summary:\*\* A high-level overview of the project and its goals.  
\*   \*\*Company Profile:\*\* A description of the client's business, industry, and why they need external consulting.  
\*   \*\*Current Situation & Challenges:\*\* Context on the current IT environment and the problem requiring a solution.

\#\# 2\. Scope of Work (SOW) & Requirements  
\*   \*\*Detailed Deliverables:\*\* Specific, actionable outcomes required, such as architectural designs, software implementations, or strategic reports.  
\*   \*\*Technical Requirements:\*\* Specific tools, systems, security standards (e.g., ISO, GDPR), and infrastructure needs.  
\*   \*\*Project Phases/Methodology:\*\* A, B, C phases of the project (e.g., Discovery, Analysis, Design, Implementation).  
\*   \*\*Out of Scope:\*\* Clear definition of what the vendor is not expected to do.

\#\# 3\. Timeline & Deliverables  
\*   \*\*Milestones:\*\* Key dates for project milestones.  
\*   \*\*Start/End Dates:\*\* The expected start date and total project duration.

\#\# 4\. Vendor Qualifications & Team Structure  
\*   \*\*Experience & Certifications:\*\* Minimum requirements for experience in similar projects.  
\*   \*\*Project Team:\*\* Request for bios or resumes of key personnel.  
\*   \*\*References:\*\* Case studies or testimonials from previous clients.

\#\# 5\. Pricing Proposal  
\*   \*\*Pricing Structure:\*\* Request for a detailed breakdown, often with a fixed price vs. Time & Materials breakdown.  
\*   \*\*Payment Milestones:\*\* Proposed milestones for invoicing (e.g., 30% kickoff, 40% midpoint, 30% completion).

\#\# 6\. Submission Guidelines & Evaluation  
\*   \*\*Proposal Guidelines:\*\* Formatting rules (e.g., PDF), page limits, and submission methods.  
\*   \*\*Contact Information:\*\* Primary contact for questions.  
\*   \*\*Evaluation Criteria:\*\* How proposals will be scored (e.g., technical capabilities, cost, experience).

\#\# 7\. Terms & Conditions  
\*   \*\*Contractual Requirements:\*\* Standard terms, including confidentiality, data handling, and service level agreements (SLAs).
