# Apex Digital Solutions — Company Profile

## Overview

Apex Digital Solutions is a boutique IT consulting firm specializing in enterprise transformation. Since 2010, we have helped mid-market and enterprise organizations modernize their technology infrastructure, implement world-class ERP systems, and build data-driven cultures.

## Our Story

Founded by three ex-Big Four consultants who were tired of watching clients pay premium rates for junior talent, Apex was built on a simple premise: **senior expertise, transparent pricing, and direct accountability.** Every project is led by a Principal Consultant with 15+ years of domain experience.

## Core Capabilities

### Cloud & Infrastructure
- Multi-cloud architecture (Azure, AWS, GCP)
- Cloud-native application development
- Infrastructure-as-Code implementations
- Cost optimization and FinOps

### Enterprise Applications
- Oracle Cloud ERP (Financials, SCM, HCM)
- SAP S/4HANA migrations
- Salesforce implementation and integration
- Microsoft Dynamics 365

### Data & Analytics
- Enterprise data warehousing
- Real-time analytics platforms
- AI/ML model deployment
- Data governance frameworks

### Cybersecurity
- Zero-trust architecture design
- Security operations center (SOC) setup
- Compliance automation (SOX, GDPR, HIPAA)
- Penetration testing and vulnerability management

## Engagement Model

We operate on three engagement models:

1. **Fixed-Price Projects** — Best for well-defined scopes with clear deliverables
2. **Time & Materials** — Ideal for discovery phases and agile transformations
3. **Managed Services** — Ongoing support with SLA-backed response times

## Client Philosophy

> "We don't just deliver systems. We deliver capability."

Every engagement includes knowledge transfer, documentation, and training. We measure our success not by go-live dates, but by how well our clients can run what we built—six months after we leave.
