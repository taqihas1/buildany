## The Problem (In 3 Bullets)

1. **Proposals take 40-60 hours each** — Senior staff hand-craft every document from scratch, hunting through email and SharePoint for past content.

2. **Quality is inconsistent** — Different people write different versions of our company story, methodology, and pricing. Clients notice.

3. **Knowledge walks out the door** — When senior staff leave, their project history, case studies, and win strategies leave with them. There is no institutional memory.

**Annual cost of this problem:** $180,000 in labor (1,200 staff hours × $150/hr blended rate) + lost deals from slow responses + damaged brand perception.

---

## The Solution (In 1 Sentence)

A lightweight, AI-powered document generation system using **OpenClaw's skill framework** — four reusable skills that store our institutional knowledge and auto-generate professional proposals, RFP responses, and procurement documents.

### What It Is

| # | Component | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Knowledge Base** (`proposal-kb`) | Stores company profile, case studies, team templates, pricing, methodology |
| 2 | **Proposal Generator** (`proposal-craft`) | Creates outbound proposals to pitch clients |
| 3 | **RFP Response Generator** (`rfp-respond`) | Creates compliant responses to client RFPs |
| 4 | **RFP Issuer** (`rfp-issue`) | Creates RFPs when we advise clients on procurement |

### How It Works (30-Second Version)

```
OPPORTUNITY → QUERY KB → GET 80% PRE-FILLED → CUSTOMIZE 20% → GENERATE DOCUMENT → DELIVER
```

**The Knowledge Base does the heavy lifting.** It remembers everything so humans don't have to.
